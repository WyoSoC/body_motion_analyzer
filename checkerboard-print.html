How to use in Motion Analyzer → Tab 1 (Calibration):
  1. Print at 100% scale – do not fit to page.
  2. Verify the scale bar (right) measures exactly 20 mm with a ruler.
  3. Place the board flat and perpendicular to the camera.
  4. Set Inner Cols = 9, Inner Rows = 6, Square Size = 20 mm.
  5. Click 2 points that span a known number of squares, enter that count, then click Calculate.
